# 2026 Goal Plan – Account Technical Leader (ATL)

## Role Overview

Account Technical Leaders (ATLs) serve as trusted technical advisors who help clients achieve
their business and technology objectives while maximizing the value of IBM solutions. They develop
and execute technical strategies aligned to client goals, lead the broader IBM technical team, and
balance two critical motions: driving new revenue through the sale of IBM products and services,
and ensuring clients realize value through the adoption and expansion of existing IBM investments.
By combining strong technical expertise, business acumen, and leadership, ATLs help clients achieve
measurable outcomes while supporting IBM's long-term growth.

---

## Goal 1: Deliver Business and Financial Results

Lead technical strategy across accounts, aligning client priorities with IBM objectives.
Progress opportunities using a consultative approach, partnering with sellers and leveraging IBM
technology to create client value.

### Key Activities
- Drive technical wins through Client Engineering engagements, demonstrations, TechZone usage,
  proofs of concept, and technical validations.
- Mitigate risk on complex or first-of-a-kind opportunities by engaging the appropriate IBM
  experts and stakeholders.
- Maintain accurate opportunity management, including ISC hygiene and timely completion of
  required technical sales activities.
- Build trusted relationships with business and technology decision-makers to influence outcomes
  and accelerate client success.
- Achieve assigned business, technical sales, adoption, and financial objectives.
- Share intellectual capital, reuse assets, and bring innovative ideas that differentiate IBM in
  the marketplace.
- Collaborate effectively across BTS, CSM, Client Engineering, TEL, Ecosystem, Services, and
  other IBM teams to deliver client outcomes.

### Measures of Success
- Achievement of assigned business, technical sales, adoption, and financial targets.
- Growth in technical wins, POC conversions, and Client Engineering engagement outcomes.
- Accurate and timely opportunity management and ISC hygiene.
- Positive feedback from sellers, clients, and cross-functional partners on technical leadership.

---

## Goal 2: Technical and Leadership Growth

Develop and execute a continuous learning plan aligned with IBM strategic priorities, client
needs, and evolving market trends.

### Key Activities
- Deepen expertise across IBM's hybrid cloud, AI, automation, data, and infrastructure portfolio
  while broadening business and industry knowledge.
- Demonstrate technical leadership through compelling client presentations, demonstrations,
  workshops, and co-creation sessions.
- Pursue and maintain relevant technical certifications, credentials, and career milestones
  aligned with role expectations.
- Build and sustain mentoring relationships as both a mentor and mentee to support personal
  growth and technical succession planning.
- Enable peers by sharing reusable assets, technical insights, best practices, and lessons
  learned from client engagements.
- Continuously strengthen leadership, communication, and influence skills to increase client
  impact and team effectiveness.

### Measures of Success
- Completion of required certifications and learning milestones within target timelines.
- Demonstrated growth in technical depth and breadth across IBM's portfolio.
- Positive feedback from clients, peers, and managers on technical leadership and communication.
- Measurable contribution to team enablement through assets, mentoring, and knowledge sharing.

---

## Goal 3: Client-Centric Execution and Team Contribution

Act as a trusted technical advisor, building credibility through preparation, accountability,
and ownership of outcomes.

### Key Activities
- Collaborate effectively with BTS, CSMs, Client Engineering, Services, Ecosystem, and other
  teams to drive client success.
- Demonstrate a growth mindset by embracing feedback, learning from challenges, and pursuing
  opportunities for innovation.
- Prioritize high-impact activities aligned with client needs and IBM strategy while managing
  time and resources effectively.
- Be bold and entrepreneurial by identifying new opportunities, taking calculated risks, and
  sharing lessons learned.
- Contribute to team success through mentoring, knowledge sharing, and development of reusable
  assets and intellectual capital.
- Demonstrate leadership through disciplined opportunity management, territory planning, and
  operational excellence.
- Foster a culture of teamwork, inclusion, and continuous improvement across the broader
  technical community.

### Measures of Success
- Positive client and stakeholder feedback on trust, responsiveness, and technical credibility.
- Demonstrated cross-functional collaboration and contribution to team outcomes.
- Evidence of growth mindset through feedback incorporation and innovation.
- Consistent operational discipline in opportunity management and territory planning.
- Active contribution to a culture of inclusion, mentoring, and knowledge sharing.
