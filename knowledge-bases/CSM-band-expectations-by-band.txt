# Performance Expectations for Customer Success Manager (CSM), by Band

Source: `CSM band-expectations.pdf` (16 April 2026).

This file restates the PDF's Factor x Band table with each cell tied to its band column. Content is taken verbatim from the PDF (line wrapping removed), except for two typos in the PDF that are corrected here ("Contirbutes" and "insights,recommenda tions"). The PDF is the authoritative source.

## Measurable outcomes for this role (all bands)

1. Achievement of Target Incentive
2. Extent to which technical activities advance deployment
3. Application of skills acquired to progress deployment / influence clients

## Performer definitions

| | Top Performer | Low Performer |
|---|---|---|
| Sales targets | Consistently achieves >100% of sales targets | Frequently falls short of meeting 100% sales targets |
| Deployment | Overachieves on extent to which technical activities advance deployment and acts as a leader who influences better business outcomes | Frequently underachieves extent to which technical activities advance deployment |
| Skills | Applies exceptional skills above the band level to progress deals | Struggles to apply acquired skills to progress deals and requires frequent support to complete tasks |
| Initiative / recognition | Earns recognition from peers and superiors | Lacking initiative and curiosity to turn challenges into opportunities |

Most IBMers meet expectations as core performers.

## Band 6 — Focuses on Individual/Team Objectives and Developing Professional Effectiveness

### Skills

- Collaborates across technical community to build deployment strategies for assigned clients
- Articulates how and why a client would utilize the IBM offering
- Demonstrates usage of IBM product capabilities to drive business outcomes
- Articulates architectural considerations for deploying products in specialization
- Demonstrates resourcefulness navigating IBM for answers

### Impact on Business / Scope

- Guides client on best practices of adoption and usage of product within specialization
- Leverages proven architectures to guide client on deployment best practices

### Contribution / Leadership

- Contributions are usually limited to assignment-related activities.
- Gains trust by being transparent & honest with others.
- Contributes to an empathetic & inclusive work environment.

## Band 7 — Focuses on Individual/Team/Department Operational Objectives

### Skills

- Performs installation of products in specialization
- Customizes proven architectures to guide client on deployment best practices specific to their environment
- Communicates technical considerations related to deployment to both technical and business sponsors effectively
- Understands and deftly handles ambiguity clients face in their business, market, industry, & technology.
- Develops and manages their own action plan to perform job duties

### Impact on Business / Scope

- Performs unassisted installation, adoption and usage of product within specialization
- Interprets customer technical requirements into business value

### Contribution / Leadership

- Provides advice in technical/operational domain of specialization.
- Engages with others to learn through feedback.
- Shares best practices with clients & teams

## Band 8 — Guides Functional Objectives or Technologies

### Skills

- Demonstrates knowledge of architectural patterns beyond IBM technology
- Performs installation of products in specialization
- Expands client adoption by showing compelling business outcomes expanding existing footprints
- Communicates effectively and successfully handles client objections when approaching deployment considerations
- Leads deployment strategy discussions for assigned specialties across the IBM team.

### Impact on Business / Scope

- Executes deployment including architecture, installation, adoption and usage spanning multiple products
- Contributes technical assets and architectures that can be repurposed and reused across multiple client engagements.
- Expands client use of IBM technology into adjacent offerings by showing an understanding of a client's business, industry, market, and technology.

### Contribution / Leadership

- Utilizes expertise to influence people outside the department or function directly.
- Becomes a coach/mentor to help others grow their career and skills.
- Challenges assumptions & seeks out divergent viewpoints

## Band 9 — Influences Functional Strategy

### Skills

- Creates architectures aligned to client needs
- Demonstrates deep technical understanding of installing and adopting products in specialization
- Demonstrates exceptional influence to build consensus, resolve conflicts, and drive decision-making within clients
- Maintains regular communication and engagement with client and partner c-suites and architects alike, ensuring long term satisfaction
- Stays ahead of industry trends providing valuable insights, recommendations internally and externally alike
- Influences client on modernization strategy

### Impact on Business / Scope

- Leads in all phases of deployment including architecture, installation, adoption and usage for complex use cases within specialty
- Identifies and creates technical assets and architectures that can be reused across multiple client engagements.
- Contributes to client strategies by showing a deep understanding of a client's business, industry, market, technology, and strategy for winning.
- Influence IBM product roadmap based on client feedback

### Contribution / Leadership

- Influences people and organizations, including executive management, when issues are complex/difficult and require considerable diplomacy.
- Recognized as a leader & sought after to coach or mentor in a particular specialization.
- Brings in a breadth of perspectives, backgrounds, & skills to teams.

## Band 10 — Leads Functional/Business Unit Strategies

### Skills

- Consistently recognized as a subject-matter-expert in all phases of deployment including architecture, installation, adoption and usage for complex use cases spanning multiple specializations
- Demonstrates transformational thought leadership on workloads and architectures across complex, hybrid cloud environments
- Sought after by product teams for deployment insights.
- Develops compelling reasons to act related to environment modernization.

### Impact on Business / Scope

- Inspires new client strategies through a thought-leadership level understanding of a client's business, industry, market, and technology.
- Advocates for clients across IBM and externally.
- Collaborates with executive-level clients on contract proposals to reach mutually beneficial outcomes.
- Influence IBM product roadmap based on market themes

### Contribution / Leadership

- Recognized as an expert and leader by members of a field, related functions, and multi-country organizations.
- Recognized as a leader & sought after to coach or mentor across specializations.
- Fosters an inclusive culture where teams feel comfortable in sharing their perspectives, backgrounds, & skills.
- Has vision of overall strategies.
