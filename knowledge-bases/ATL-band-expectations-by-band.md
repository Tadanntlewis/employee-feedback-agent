# Performance Expectations for Account Technical Leader (ATL), by Band

Source: `ATL IBM Performance Expectations By Band 2026.pdf` (IBM Confidential, March 2026).

This file restates the PDF's Factor x Band table with each cell tied to its band column. Content is taken verbatim from the PDF (line wrapping removed). The PDF is the authoritative source.

## Important: Bands 6 and 7

The PDF's table has columns for Bands 6 and 7, but **those columns are empty** for all three factors (Skills, Business Impact, Behaviors); the only marks in them are two stray, empty bullet characters under Behaviors. Expectations are defined only for **Bands 8, 9 and 10**. Band 9 states "Band 8 expectations plus", and Band 10 states "Band 9 expectations plus", so the expectations are cumulative.

## Measurable outcomes for this role (all bands)

1. Achievement of Target Incentive
2. Near- and long-term opportunity creation and pursuit as part of a Client Technical Strategy
3. Application of skills acquired to progress deployment / influence clients

## Performer definitions

| | Top Performer | Low Performer |
|---|---|---|
| Sales targets | Consistently achieves >100% of sales targets | Frequently falls short of meeting 100% sales targets |
| Skills | Applies exceptional skills above the band level to progress deals | Struggles to apply acquired skills to progress deals and requires frequent support to complete tasks |
| Initiative / recognition | Earns recognition from peers and superiors | Lacks initiative and curiosity to turn challenges into opportunities |

Most IBMers meet expectations as core performers.

## Band 8 — Guides Functional Objectives or Technologies

### Skills

Drives Client Technical Strategy based on:

- broad technical expertise across the IBM Technology platforms focusing on technical capabilities and functionality; attains required L3 and L4 badges
- broad knowledge of competitor offerings and the advantages of IBM Technologies relative to them
- broad knowledge of horizontal and vertical industries relevant to their client set

### Business Impact

- Maps clients’ business and technology objectives to IBM Technology solutions.
- Collaborates with the IBM account team (TSR, BTS, CSM, CE, TEL, etc.) to create and execute a Client Technical Strategy that drives near-, mid-, and long-term opportunities, revenue, and deployments (as measured by SI dashboard)
- Activates IBM Client Engagement Model to drive deployment of IBM software
- Builds relationships with key technology and business stakeholders and business leaders within their client set.
- Effectively communicates IBM PoVs and product perspectives to all levels of clients’ organizations

### Behaviors

- Runs weekly account technical team standup meeting
- Works collaboratively with their account team to set direction, manage execution, and achieve individual and account-level targets.
- Identifies and effectively applies sales and solution assets (IBM PoV’s, reference architectures, solution architectures, etc.) to their client set
- Contributes to internal education and knowledge sharing events
- Maintains a presence on social media; highlighting technology trends and directions

## Band 9 — Influences Functional Strategy

### Skills

Band 8 expectations plus: Drives Client Technical Strategy based on:

- deep technical expertise in one technical topics or subject areas.
- deep knowledge of relevant horizontal and vertical industries, and the impact of regulatory requirements, industry trends, etc. to their clients

### Business Impact

- Creates solutions (people, process, and technology) using IBM Technology to address clients’ business and technical objectives
- Builds ongoing relationships with senior technology and business leaders within their client set.
- Effectively communicates the client value of IBM Technology solutions to all levels of clients’ organizations

### Behaviors

- Leads and coaches their account team in the planning and execution of technical sales activities, and a larger client technical strategy.
- Contributes to the refinement and/or creation of sales and solution assets
- Regularly presents at and contributes to the planning and organization of internal education and knowledge sharing events
- Presents as a topic SME and/or IBM representative to client-/industry-focused external events.
- Actively comments on technology topics and content to advance conversations and show implications to clients and industries

## Band 10 — Leads Functional/Business Unit Strategies

### Skills

Band 9 expectations plus: Drives Client Technical Strategy based on:

- deep technical expertise in two technical topics or subject areas.
- ‘expert’ level knowledge of their clients’ industry and the impact of industry trends, competitive threats, regulatory changes, etc. on their clients.

### Business Impact

- Creates complex business and technology solutions that address clients’ and industries’ most strategic objectives.
- Viewed as a trusted advisor to executive and senior business and technical leaders within their client set.
- Formulates and effectively communicates IBM PoV’s on technology directions and the opportunities /threats it represents to the client.

### Behaviors

- Effectively leads a large account team to balance workload, assist team members to achieve targets, and drive overall account growth.
- Provides constructive feedback and ongoing mentoring as part of the CTS review process
- Identifies / creates and advocates the appropriate application of sales and solution assets.
- Presents as a topic SME and IBM representative to large client and/or industry events
- Maintains a social media presence as a thought-leader; publishes original content on technology and/or business topics.
