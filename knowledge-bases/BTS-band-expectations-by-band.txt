# Performance Expectations for Client Engineering – Brand Technical Specialist (BTS), by Band

Source: `BTS band-expectations.pdf` (16 April 2026, IBM Confidential).

This file restates the PDF's Factor x Band table with each cell tied to its band column. Content is taken verbatim from the PDF (line wrapping removed). The PDF is the authoritative source.

## Measurable outcomes for this role (all bands)

1. Achievement of Target Incentive
2. Meets Technical Sales KPIs
3. Application of skills to reduce post-close technical issues and rework

## Performer definitions

| | Top Performer | Low Performer |
|---|---|---|
| Sales targets | Consistently achieves >100% of sales targets | Frequently falls short of meeting 100% sales targets |
| Skills | Applies exceptional skills above the band level to progress deals | Struggles to apply acquired skills to progress deals and requires frequent support to complete tasks |
| Initiative / recognition | Earns recognition from peers and superiors | Lacking initiative and curiosity to turn challenges into opportunities |

Most IBMers meet expectations as core performers.

## Cumulative expectations and Band 10

Bands 7, 8 and 9 are written as "plus" statements (for example Band 7: "B06, plus: ..."), so each band includes all lower-band expectations. The PDF marks **Band 10 as "Exception use only"**, and states that higher band Solution Architects are by exception only. Band 10 has no defined expectations (Skills shows "n/a").

## Band 6 — Focuses on Individual/Team Objectives and Developing Professional Effectiveness

### Skills

- Completes all required on-boarding training
- Successfully completes mentor engagement for first 6 months in role
- Performs demos for Focus Products
- Participates in Territory Planning
- Utilizes Digital tools for customer outreach
- Understands client needs as they align to IBM Solution
- Learns fundamental Brand product knowledge and applies to opportunities

### Business Impact

- Successfully completes onboarding requirements
- Partner first strategy for all opportunities

### Behaviors

- Leverage digital tools to build social presence

## Band 7 — Focuses on Individual/Team/Department Operational Objectives

### Skills

B06, plus:

- Meets and demonstrates technical sales and brand skill expectation
- Develops and manages their own action plan or system to effectively perform job duties
- Uses elevated communication skills to effectively engage with clients and colleagues to close territory opportunities
- Conducts unassisted client engagements for Focus Products (architecture, demo, and pilots)
- Enables effective client engagements and solution design within brand solutions
- Proactively manages client opportunities and collaborates with partners through the sales cycle to close deals, fostering autonomy to scale business growth

### Business Impact

B06 plus:

- Proactively provides webinar’s / workshops to drive opportunities from qualification to proposal, showcasing IBM’s technology
- Meets Tech Sales KPIs as defined

### Behaviors

B06 plus:

- Continuously refines and improves sales assets for repeatability
- Leverage digital tools to build relationships with clients, prospects, and industry peers

## Band 8 — Guides Functional Objectives or Technologies

### Skills

B07, plus:

- Stays current and demonstrates deep understanding of relevant technologies, enabling guidance and consultation to clients and peers
- Collaborates with internal teams, such as product specialists or engineers, to align technical solutions towards client need
- Proactively develops reasons to call clients and partners, assisting in increasing partner technical capacity within territory
- Develops and delivers engaging and informative presentations, demos, and workshops progressing deals thru to usage

### Business Impact

B07 plus:

- Demonstrates the capability to carry and exceed increased individual quotas
- Increases POX autonomy
- Designs effective territory strategies to achieve quota targets, continuously refining approaches based on market conditions and client needs

### Behaviors

B07 plus:

- Demonstrates increased leadership skills, leading and guiding new/junior members of the team towards success, mentoring
- Independently develops high-quality sales assets that are repurposed and reused across multiple client engagements
- Leverages social media platforms to engage with clients, build relationships, and promote solutions

## Band 9 — Influences Functional Strategy

### Skills

B08, plus:

- Stays ahead of industry trends providing valuable insights and recommendations internally and externally
- Leads by example, a clear champion for IBM, demonstrating exceptional influence skills, guiding all teams through action and strategy creation
- Demonstrates exceptional influence to build consensus, resolve conflicts, and drive decision-making within clients; supported by partner autonomy
- Shares knowledge and expertise with clients and partners, positioning the company as a true trusted advisor and thought leader; regular communication and engagement with c-suite at clients and partners

### Business Impact

B08 plus:

- Demonstrates strong communication and influence, providing guidance and support to leaders within the BU
- Develops and delivers training programs and resources to help others achieve role excellence
- Drives individual quotas for strategic clients and / or market quotas

### Behaviors

B08 plus:

- Demonstrates strong leadership skills, providing guidance, support, and mentorship to the entire business unit
- Ensures assets are repurposed and reused across multiple client engagements and opportunities, maximizing their value and impact
- Establishes a strong professional presence on social media platforms, showcasing expertise and thought leadership
