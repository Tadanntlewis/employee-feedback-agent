## Instructions for Managers

This self-assessment should be:
1. **Requested** at least 1 week before the performance review meeting
2. **Reviewed** by the manager before the review discussion
3. **Used** to inform the performance review conversation
4. **Considered** alongside manager observations and feedback
5. **Kept confidential** and stored according to HR policies

The self-assessment provides valuable employee perspective and should be incorporated into the final performance review where appropriate.
