## Instructions for Completion

1. **Be Comprehensive**: This is your annual review - take time to reflect on the full year
2. **Be Specific**: Provide concrete examples, metrics, and outcomes
3. **Be Honest**: Share both successes and areas where you faced challenges
4. **Be Strategic**: Think about how your work aligns with organizational goals
5. **Be Forward-Looking**: Consider your career trajectory and development needs
6. **Use Evidence**: Reference specific projects, metrics, feedback, or achievements

## Submission

Once completed, please submit this form to your manager at least one week before your scheduled year-end review meeting to allow adequate time for preparation.
